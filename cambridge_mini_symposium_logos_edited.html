Aims for the Day

An informal mini symposium rather than a formal conference. Short talks are starting points for discussion, with time reserved for identifying shared interests, datasets, methods, possible grants, and future collaborative projects.

  • Share current and ongoing work across groups
  • Give ECRs an opportunity to present developing projects
  • Identify overlapping interests across labs
  • Discuss collaborative analyses, grants, papers, and data-sharing opportunities
  • Build connections between Cambridge and UCL groups

Five threads to pull on together

Possible Discussion Themes

Starting points for the breakout and roundtable conversations — areas where our methods, data, and questions already overlap.

i
Ageing, similarity, and brain structure / function
Potential overlap between similarity-based ageing work, neuroimaging markers, and trajectories of brain ageing.
ii
Methodological approaches
Computational modelling approaches used across the groups, possible applications, datasets where they could be useful, and links to current projects — including shared interests in normative modelling, brain-age models, similarity metrics, multimodal imaging, and reproducible pipelines.
iii
Neurodegeneration and clinical translation
How ongoing work could connect to dementia, Parkinsonian disorders, cognitive decline, or other clinical cohorts.
iv
Cross-cohort analyses and data harmonisation
Opportunities to compare methods or findings across Cambridge, UCL, and other datasets.
v
Collaborative projects and grants
Potential funding calls, short-term pilot analyses, ECR-led projects, joint papers, or larger collaborative grant ideas.
